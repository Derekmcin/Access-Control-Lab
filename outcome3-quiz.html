How to play

  1. Type your first name and pick a mode: Quick Fire, Full Mission or Practice.
  2. Read the question. The yellow How to answer line under each question tells you exactly what to tap.
  3. One-tap questions (multiple choice, true or false, diagram) are marked as soon as you tap. Select-all, matching and ordering questions are marked when you tap Check.
  4. Green ACCESS GRANTED means right. Red ACCESS DENIED means wrong. Either way you see the correct answer and why. Tap Next to carry on.
  5. At the end you get your rank (Visitor, Keyholder, Supervisor or Master Key), the topics to revise, and a results card to screenshot for your lecturer.

Points: 100 per right answer. Streak: +10 bonus for each answer in a row (up to +50). Quick Fire: up to +50 extra for answering fast.

Full Mission: 3 or more right out of 5 unlocks a door (1 to 3 stars). Shields protect your streak from a wrong answer.

The timer pauses while this box is open. Open it again any time with the ? button at the top.

Choose your mode

Quick Fire

10 random questions against the clock (20 to 45 seconds each). Faster correct answers earn up to 50 bonus points. If the timer runs out, it counts as wrong.

Full Mission

40 questions: 8 doors (one per topic), 5 questions each. Get 3 or more right to unlock a door and earn up to 3 stars. No timer.

You have 3 shields. A shield takes the hit for a wrong answer so your streak is not lost. You win a shield back for every 5 right in a row.

Practice

No timer, no pressure. Choose one topic or all of them, and see the answer and explanation after each question. Tap End whenever you want to stop.

Points: 100 for each right answer, plus a streak bonus of +10 for every answer in a row (up to +50). Your rank at the end depends on your %.
